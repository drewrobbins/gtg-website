Our Story

40 Years of Craft. One Unwavering Standard.

From the dining rooms of Pensacola to the kitchen of 30A's finest gourmet-to-go operation - this is the story of Chef Gary Serafin.

The Chef

Chef Gary Serafin

Chef Gary Serafin spent 17 years as owner and executive chef of Jamie's Restaurant in Pensacola - a beloved institution known for its meticulous technique and uncompromising ingredients. Alongside him, Sous Chef Derek Osborne built a kitchen culture where shortcuts were simply not permitted.

When Gary transitioned to Emerald Coast Gourmet To Go and Emerald Coast Catering, he brought every standard with him. The same knife work. The same sourcing philosophy. The same belief that food either honors the people eating it, or it doesn't.

With 40+ years of professional kitchen experience, Gary's food reflects what happens when mastery meets purpose.

3 Pillar Health Partnership

Where Culinary Mastery Meets Metabolic Medicine

In 2026, Emerald Coast Gourmet To Go partnered with Drew Robbins and 3 Pillar Health to create the Florida Panhandle's first clinically-aligned gourmet meal service.

Drew Robbins is a Metabolic Health Practitioner with over 12 years of practice in the Santa Rosa Beach area and a nationwide Metabolic Telehealth Practice. His patients needed more than "healthy" food — they needed food that matched their therapeutic protocols. Chef Gary's commitment to real ingredients and honest cooking made the partnership an obvious fit.

The result: the 3P Approved tier system - five dietary categories that let you order with clinical confidence, not just marketing hope.
